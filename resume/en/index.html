Research Experience

MentalHospital

NeurIPS 2026

Evaluating LLMs through simulated psychiatric interviews

Lead, patient simulation & system framework

  • Led the core SkillPatient patient simulator, combining staged clinical-state modeling with search and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). This grounds simulated patient responses in clinical knowledge while maintaining contextual consistency and realism throughout extended, multi-turn interviews.
  • Developed the staged framework and core system, organizing each clinical interview into a modular, multi-stage pipeline. Built the environment and experimental pipelines supporting the execution and scoring of all evaluated models.
  • Provided a reproducible testbed for interactive, multi-turn, high-stakes evaluation, a setting largely missing from existing benchmarks.

ToolWorld

Manuscript in preparation

Tool-integration reasoning in open tool ecosystems

Co-first author

  • Co-designed and built a benchmark in which long-horizon tasks run in an open tool ecosystem with real, uncurated tools and tool failures during execution, rather than a sandbox of curated tools.
  • Designed a fine-grained evaluation layer. Trajectory-level diagnostics measure both end-to-end task success and an agent’s detection, localization, and repair of individual tool failures.
  • Co-conducted large-scale experiments on leading LLM agents, identifying three patterns: a substantial performance gap between curated tools and open ecosystems; local repairs rarely translate into full task completion; and early unresolved failures compound over long-horizon tasks.
